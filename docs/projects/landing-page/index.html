Sleepal DTC Website

From validating the proposition to supporting direct sales.

0. Project overview

Goal

Help people understand it,
and want to buy it.

Iterated product storytelling, conversion paths and the purchase experience across validation, pre-launch, crowdfunding and launch.

My role

Product & experience design.
Technical delivery.

Used campaign feedback to refine content and pages. Researched APIs and built demos to turn business needs into actionable plans with engineering.

Outcome

From a pre-launch page
to a direct-sales website

Refined lead capture and crowdfunding handoffs. After launch, shifted the story from technical features to emotional experience, while improving purchasing and tracking.

1. Validation: find what matters to users

Early testing changed how I framed the product. Using customer profiles and campaign feedback, I shifted the page from “what a smart lamp can do” to “how it helps me sleep better,” then supported those benefits with technology and research.

Look beyond the click

From validation to pre-launch, click-through rate rose and cost per click fell, but page conversion also declined. I focused on missing decision-making information and the post-click journey, while continuing to compare audiences and creatives.

Validation and pre-launch performance

Validation: Dec 2025–Jan 2026; pre-launch: Mar 2026. Spend, audiences and creatives also changed, so these differences cannot be attributed to the page redesign alone.

2. Pre-launch: turn interest into reservations

Added demos, device comparisons, press coverage and FAQs to answer “is it for me?”, “why trust it?” and “what happens next?”. Refined the handoff between lead capture, thank-you and deposit pages.

Make reservation terms clear

Explained how the offer, deposit and final purchase fit together, reducing confusion about payment and delivery and clarifying the next step after signup.

Updated page content and mobile entry points together so visitors did not have to relearn the same rules on each page.

3. Crowdfunding: refine the path and tracking

Traffic moved from ads through the website to Kickstarter, where source information could be lost. I helped investigate parameter handling and attribution, weighing purchase friction against traceability with engineering.
